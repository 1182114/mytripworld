# My Trip World — How to update your website

Open **https://mytripworld.sanity.studio** (or type `mytripworld.net/admin`) and sign in.
Everything you change goes live on the website about 1–2 minutes after you press **Publish**.

> Tip (Hindi): Jo bhi badlein, neeche hare **Publish** button ko dabana zaroori hai.
> Publish ke 1–2 minute baad website par dikhega.

## See new enquiries

1. Click **Enquiries → New**.
2. Open an enquiry to see the name, phone, trip and message.
3. After calling the customer, change **Status** (Contacted / Quote sent / Booked) and
   add **Your notes**. Press **Publish** to save.

## Change a price

1. **Packages → All packages** → open the package.
2. Click the **Price** tab.
3. Change **Offer price (₹)** – numbers only, e.g. `99999`.
   Optional: **Old price** (shown crossed out) and **Price terms** (per person, twin sharing, valid till).
4. Press **Publish**.

The price updates by itself on the package page, the cards, the footer and the Google
price data. But if the old price is also *typed inside a sentence* — the package's
**Search description** (FAQ & Google tab), a **FAQ** answer, or the **Home page → About
block** text — change it there too.

## Add a new package

1. **Packages → All packages** → click the **+** (pencil) button at the top.
2. **Basics** tab: package name → click **Generate** next to *Web address* → type of
   package → countries/places (one at a time) → nights and days → short description.
3. **Price** tab: offer price and price terms.
4. **Itinerary & inclusions** tab: what is included / not included, and one entry per day.
5. **Photos** tab: upload the main photo and describe it in one line. Add more photos if you have them.
6. Tick **Show on the home page** if you want it among the top packages.
7. Press **Publish**.

## Add a departure date

1. Open the package → **Departure dates** tab → **Add item**.
2. Pick the date, type the seats left, press **Publish**. Past dates disappear automatically.

## Add a customer review

1. **Customer reviews** → **+**.
2. Type the customer's name, which trip, and **their real words**.
   Choose where the review came from (Google, WhatsApp, told us directly…).
3. Optional: stars and a photo of the customer.
4. Press **Publish**.

Only add reviews that real customers gave you. Made-up reviews can get the website
penalised by Google and can cause legal trouble.

## Add a gallery photo

**Gallery → +** → upload the photo → describe it → type where it was taken → **Publish**.

## Change phone number, WhatsApp, email or office address

**Site settings** → **Phone & email** or **Offices** tab → change → **Publish**.
The new details appear everywhere on the website (header, footer, contact page, forms).

## Add or change an FAQ

**FAQs** → open one or press **+** → type the question and answer → **Publish**.

## Run an offer

**Offers → +** → title, details, linked package and the **Valid till** date → **Publish**.
The offer shows on the home page and disappears by itself after the date.

## Good to know

- **Sort order**: lower numbers show first.
- **Photos**: use wide photos of at least 1600 pixels. Always fill in "Describe this photo".
- **Undo**: open the document → the clock icon (History) shows older versions you can restore.
- **Do not change a "Web address"** once a page is live — old links would stop working.
- If the website does not update within 5 minutes, a required field is probably empty.
  Tell your developer; nothing on the live site is broken in the meantime.
